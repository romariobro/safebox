How it works

A clear structure.
At every step.

A physical case, visible labels and accessible information form one system.

A simple path

From intention to an organised kit.

01 / SAFEBOX

Choose

Adult or family: start with a simple choice, without a long questionnaire.

02 / SAFEBOX

Discuss

Agree on your supplies and individual prescriptions with a healthcare professional.

03 / SAFEBOX

Organise

Obtain agreed supplies, label the sections and show your family where to find them.

Find the section.
Read the label.

Use colour, symbols and text together. Keep medicines identifiable in their original packaging and retain the manufacturer’s instructions.

The illustrated sections are a navigation concept, not a treatment algorithm. Individual use follows professional advice.
