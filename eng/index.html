The founder’s personal experience

The story, honestly told

3–5 minutes

And everything changed

My wife initially vomited several times over a few hours. Then, within 3–5 minutes, her condition worsened sharply: her limbs cramped, her mouth became numb and breathing became difficult.

Read the full story

Doctors later attributed the episode to severe dehydration. At the time, I was frightened and trying to understand what to do. We were outside a major city. When I called the ambulance service, the operator told me to wait for an available crew.

While searching our medicine kit, I decided to give her Zyrtec. She began to feel better later, but that sequence does not establish that the medicine caused the improvement. The ambulance arrived about half an hour later and the doctors treated the continuing nausea.

That experience became the starting point for SAFEBOX: clear storage and accessible individual instructions, so the whole household knows where to find them.

One night

A child’s sudden nighttime illness

One night, my son suddenly began coughing intensely and could not stop. It was frightening to watch. We already had a nebuliser and Pulmicort at home, bought three years earlier on a doctor’s advice.

I still wonder: what if we had had nothing at hand? This episode, which I describe as croup, reinforced why preparation should not be put off.

For me, it is a reminder to discuss supplies with a clinician in advance, know where they are and keep current individual instructions accessible.

That is how SAFEBOX began: clearly organised supplies and important information within reach.

Personal recollections, not treatment instructions. Improvement after a medicine does not establish its cause. Check expiry dates and follow current medical advice. Breathing difficulty requires urgent medical help.

Before you choose

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Is SAFEBOX a medicine or a storage system?

SAFEBOX is a concept for organising medicines and related information. Medicines are selected individually with a healthcare professional and purchased separately.

Is it suitable for someone without medical training?

The navigation is designed to make storage easier to understand. It does not replace first-aid training, a diagnosis or professional medical help.

Can I buy it and order medicines in one click?

Sales, payments and pharmacy ordering are not connected yet. The current site helps you explore the concept and prepare a checklist.

What if there is no phone or internet?

The concept includes printed information. Online videos and QR resources need an internet connection. Keep your individual prescriptions and key contacts available offline.
