SAFEBOX

Free digital guide

Basic edition: storage organisation and preparation. Free to download and print.

1. Choose a location

Follow storage conditions on the packaging. Keep medicines inaccessible to children. Store items with special requirements separately as instructed.

2. Make an inventory

Record the name, form, pack quantity and expiry date. Retain original packaging and instructions. Do not use unidentified or unlabelled products.

3. Review the list

Discuss adult and child supplies, current prescriptions and restrictions with a clinician. This guide does not provide universal treatments or doses.

4. Organise sections

Use clear section labels. Show other adults where supplies, prescriptions and contacts are kept. Colour supports navigation, not treatment decisions.

5. Review and replenish

Set a review date. Track use, shelf life after opening and replenishment needs. Confirm pharmacy prices at the time of ordering.
