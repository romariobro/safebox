Caring for children

Care begins
with attention.

A proposed range of play-doctor sets and a future programme to support children’s homes.

01 / SAFEBOX

Learning through play

The idea is to help families explore care and the role of a doctor through age-appropriate play.

02 / SAFEBOX

Age suitability

The concept mentions a 1+ range. Final age labelling and safety requirements must be verified before launch.

03 / SAFEBOX

Separate from medicines

Play sets are toys. Children must not have free access to real medicines or medical supplies.

A future giving programme

Purchases with a wider purpose.

We plan to allocate part of eligible sales to support children’s homes. The amount, participating products, beneficiary organisation and reporting process will be announced before the programme begins.

The programme is in development. Contributions are not currently being made under the programme described here.
